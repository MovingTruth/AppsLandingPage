◎ The foundation beneath every app

Human first. Private by design. Accessible from the beginning.

Every Moving Truth app begins with the same permanent foundation: human dignity, ethical restraint, understandable privacy, and technology that serves the person holding it. Beauty must make function feel natural—not obscure it.

  • Accessibility is a release requirement. Every Apple-platform app is designed and tested against Apple’s applicable accessibility guidance throughout its life—not treated as a separate edition or a final polish pass.
  • Fourteen languages, everywhere. Every current and future Moving Truth app begins with the same fourteen-language architecture so people across the world receive the whole experience, not a translated fragment.
  • Privacy and ethics are structural. No person’s inner life should become the product. Data collection, permissions, business models, notifications, and new technology must remain honest, proportionate, and under human control.
  • iOS is the reference expression. Some apps will also have a native Android counterpart with the same human, privacy, accessibility, ethical, and language commitments. It will be built honestly for Android rather than presented as an identical visual copy: some of the depth, material, motion, and system integration possible on Apple platforms cannot be reproduced there in exactly the same way.
