# Moving Truth Apps Landing Page

The public home of the Moving Truth app family at `apps.movingtruth.com`.

## Product sources

Before changing an app's claims or direction, read its own repository instructions and
creative charter. The current family includes Inner Whisper, Inner Thought, Inner Sign,
and Inner Course.

## Rules

- Preserve the living-canvas harmony shared by Inner Whisper and Inner Thought.
- Let each app keep its own identity while remaining visibly part of one family.
- Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts, sufficient contrast,
  and a complete reduced-motion experience.
- Make no unverified accessibility, privacy, price, availability, or medical claims.
- No trackers, analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, accounts, or third-party SDKs.
- Do not publish without Robin's explicit approval.
